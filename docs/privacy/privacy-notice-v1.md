# ONE privacy notice v1 — draft for review

**This text is a draft. It must be adapted, translated, made accessible, and approved by the controller and privacy counsel before use.**

## What ONE is for

ONE helps an authorized household review recent, approximate observations such as where a supported object was last seen. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, identify people, score emotions, or make emergency or medical decisions.

## What may be processed

Depending on the controls you choose: account and role details; camera/device pairing data; a room map and calibration; short-lived camera frames processed in memory; approximate object observations; optional short event clips; check-in text; generated summaries; and security records.

## Your choices

Live video, audio, clips, assistant transcripts, caregiver sharing, and any future cloud processing must be separate purposes. Camera and microphone start off. You can pause processing, withdraw a purpose, and request access, correction, restriction, objection, portability, or deletion through the household operator.

## Important limits

Locations are approximate and can be wrong. A low-confidence result is shown as a room or camera zone. Do not use ONE as the sole basis for a medical, emergency, or similarly consequential decision. Do not place cameras in bathrooms or other highly private areas.

## Sharing and retention

The MVP is designed for a same-LAN deployment. Live media is transient; optional clips default to seven days, event/observation metadata to 30 days, and summaries to 30 days. Actual recipients, hosting, backups, and retention must be listed by the controller before activation.

## Contact and representation

Insert the controller identity, contact, DPO/contact point, supervisory authority route, and the process for informing and representing a resident with limited capacity. Caregiver status alone does not establish authority to consent.

## Changes

Record this notice as `v1`, show the effective date, and obtain a new purpose-specific decision where a material change affects the resident.
